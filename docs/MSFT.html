Ten-year breaks

  • Jun 2017: Acquired LinkedIn Corporation on 2016-12-08 for $27.00B (consideration), 14% of total assets at the prior year end.
  • Jun 2017: Operating income $29.02B as later restated; first reported as $22.33B (+30.0%) in the 10-K filed 2017-08-02.
  • Jun 2017: Pre-tax income $29.90B as later restated; first reported as $23.15B (+29.2%) in the 10-K filed 2017-08-02.
  • Jun 2019: ASC 606 (revenue) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2017-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • Jun 2020: ASC 842 (leases) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2018-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • Jun 2024: Acquired Activision Blizzard, Inc on 2023-10-13 for $75.40B (consideration), 18% of total assets at the prior year end.

Breaks are marked, never cut out: a year after a large acquisition, a new accounting standard or a restatement may not compare cleanly with the years before it. An acquisition counts when its price is at least 10% of the buyer's total assets (SEC Regulation S-X Rule 1-02(w), the investment test). Where a later 10-K restated a year, the restated figure is shown; years before a restated stretch stay as first reported.
