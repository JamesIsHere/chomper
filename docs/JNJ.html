Ten-year breaks

  • 2017: Acquired Actelion on 2017-06-16 for $30.05B (net assets acquired), 21% of total assets at the prior year end.
  • 2018: ASC 606 (revenue) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2017-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • 2019: ASC 842 (leases) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2018-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • Jan 2022: Pre-tax income $19.18B as later restated; first reported as $22.78B (-15.8%) in the 10-K filed 2022-02-17.
  • Jan 2022: Revenue $78.74B as later restated; first reported as $93.78B (-16.0%) in the 10-K filed 2022-02-17.
  • Jan 2023: Pre-tax income $19.36B as later restated; first reported as $21.73B (-10.9%) in the 10-K filed 2023-02-16.
  • Jan 2023: Revenue $79.99B as later restated; first reported as $94.94B (-15.7%) in the 10-K filed 2023-02-16.

Breaks are marked, never cut out: a year after a large acquisition, a new accounting standard or a restatement may not compare cleanly with the years before it. An acquisition counts when its price is at least 10% of the buyer's total assets (SEC Regulation S-X Rule 1-02(w), the investment test). Where a later 10-K restated a year, the restated figure is shown; years before a restated stretch stay as first reported.
