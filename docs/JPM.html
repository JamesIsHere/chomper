Ten-year breaks

  • 2016: Revenue $96.57B as later restated; first reported as $95.67B (+0.9%) in the 10-K filed 2017-02-28.
  • 2017: Revenue $100.70B as later restated; first reported as $99.62B (+1.1%) in the 10-K filed 2018-02-27.
  • 2018: ASC 606 (revenue) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2017-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • 2018: Revenue $108.78B as later restated; first reported as $109.03B (-0.2%) in the 10-K filed 2019-02-26.
  • 2019: ASC 842 (leases) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2018-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • 2019: Pre-tax income $44.87B as later restated; first reported as $44.55B (+0.7%) in the 10-K filed 2020-02-25.
  • 2019: Revenue $115.72B as later restated; first reported as $115.63B (+0.1%) in the 10-K filed 2020-02-25.
  • 2020: Pre-tax income $35.81B as later restated; first reported as $35.41B (+1.2%) in the 10-K filed 2021-02-23.
  • 2020: Revenue $119.95B as later restated; first reported as $119.54B (+0.3%) in the 10-K filed 2021-02-23.

Breaks are marked, never cut out: a year after a large acquisition, a new accounting standard or a restatement may not compare cleanly with the years before it. An acquisition counts when its price is at least 10% of the buyer's total assets (SEC Regulation S-X Rule 1-02(w), the investment test). Where a later 10-K restated a year, the restated figure is shown; years before a restated stretch stay as first reported.
