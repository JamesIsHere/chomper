Ten-year breaks

  • 2017: Operating income $1.95B as later restated; first reported as $771.1M (+153.4%) in the 10-K filed 2018-02-15.
  • 2018: Acquired DCT Industrial Trust Inc and DCT Industrial Operating Partnership LP on 2018-08-22 for $8.50B (consideration), 29% of total assets at the prior year end.
  • 2018: ASC 606 (revenue) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2017-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • 2018: Operating income $1.69B as later restated; first reported as $847.0M (+99.3%) in the 10-K filed 2019-02-13.
  • 2019: ASC 842 (leases) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2018-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • 2020: Acquired Liberty property trust and liberty property limited partnership on 2020-02-04 for $13.00B (consideration), 32% of total assets at the prior year end.
  • 2022: Acquired Duke realty corporation and duke realty limited partnership on 2022-10-03 for $23.20B (consideration), 40% of total assets at the prior year end.

Breaks are marked, never cut out: a year after a large acquisition, a new accounting standard or a restatement may not compare cleanly with the years before it. An acquisition counts when its price is at least 10% of the buyer's total assets (SEC Regulation S-X Rule 1-02(w), the investment test). Where a later 10-K restated a year, the restated figure is shown; years before a restated stretch stay as first reported.
