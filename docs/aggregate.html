Costs and revenue across the S&P 500

Revenue and pre-tax income summed across 421 of today's S&P 500 companies, 2016 to 2025, first for the whole index and then for each sector. Costs are revenue less pre-tax income. Sums are dominated by the largest companies. Each company's figures come from its own 10-K filings. Its latest fiscal year is placed in the calendar year it most nearly covers: a year ending on or before 30 June counts as the calendar year before, one ending 1 July or later as the year it ends in. Each earlier year sits one step before it, so a 52- or 53-week year that drifts across 30 June keeps its place.

The S&P 500 as it was, against today’s members

As it was (the members on 1 January of each year): revenue +6.4% a year, costs +6.1% a year. Today’s members traced back: revenue +6.9% a year, costs +6.5% a year. The chart shows each one’s pre-tax margin (pre-tax income as a share of revenue).
