ASC 606 and 842 adopted without recasting
2026-10-30

A company that adopted ASC 606 (2018) or ASC 842 (2019) by the modified retrospective method never recast earlier years, so the ten-year series changes basis that year and no 10-K shows both bases. The one-basis rule cannot detect it. The page marks each standard in the year it became required, not the year adopted, and not the method: XBRL adoption tagging is inconsistent (PGR and PLD tag none; UNH tags 606 in FY2016).

The ten-year headline promises one accounting basis; in these years the change row looks clean but may not be.

Companies: MSFT: 606 consistent with a fully retrospective adoption (the FY2017 recast), adoption note not read. AAPL, CAT, HD, JNJ, JPM, ODFL, PGR, PLD, UNH: method not read.

Due: before the full S&P 500 run, or before the headline is called complete.

2026-11-01

Only deals of at least 10% of prior total assets are marked. A serial acquirer making many smaller deals reads as organic growth; the run only stops when unexplained acquisition spend in a single year reaches 10%.

The headline marks acquisitions so readers know when growth is bought; many small deals add up to the same thing.

Companies: all; none checked for cumulative small deals.

Due: before the full S&P 500 run.

2026-11-01

The ten-year headline promises the same accounting basis, not the same business. Organic growth would come from each deal's ASC 805 pro forma disclosure (revenue and earnings as if acquired at the start of the prior year), read and approved company by company.

Reported growth after a large deal mixes bought and grown revenue.

Companies: MSFT, HD, JNJ, PLD (the companies with significant deals): pro formas not read.

Due: when a reader needs organic growth, or when I1 starts.

Ten-year chart depends on the first year
2026-11-01

The chart shows each line as a multiple of the first year. When that year is unusually small, the line explodes and the other line goes flat: CAT's 2016 pre-tax income was $139.0M, so pre-tax income reads 83x by 2025 and revenue (1.75x) is a flat line at the bottom. A negative first year drops the chart entirely.

The chart's shape then reflects one odd year, not the decade.

Companies: CAT (83x); PGR (pre-tax 0.63x to 9.67x) stretched; others read normally. At 500 companies, more.
