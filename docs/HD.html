Ten-year breaks

  • Feb 2019: ASC 606 (revenue) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2017-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • Feb 2020: ASC 842 (leases) required: the first fiscal year beginning after 2018-12-15. The company may have adopted it earlier.
  • Jan 2021: Acquired HD Holdings Inc on 2020-12-24 for $8.70B (consideration), 17% of total assets at the prior year end.
  • Feb 2025: Acquired SRS Acquisition on 2024-06-18 for $18.03B (consideration), 24% of total assets at the prior year end.

Breaks are marked, never cut out: a year after a large acquisition, a new accounting standard or a restatement may not compare cleanly with the years before it. An acquisition counts when its price is at least 10% of the buyer's total assets (SEC Regulation S-X Rule 1-02(w), the investment test). Where a later 10-K restated a year, the restated figure is shown; years before a restated stretch stay as first reported.
