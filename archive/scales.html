Trial page, not linked from the site. The ten-year chart in dollars on a log scale, with the cost gap as bars under it.

Lines: dollars on a log scale, so equal distances up the axis are equal percentage changes. Bars: the pre-tax income the company would have earned at the prior year's pre-tax margin, minus what it earned. A bar above zero means costs grew faster than revenue that year, by that many dollars.
